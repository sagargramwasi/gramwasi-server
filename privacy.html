GramWasi — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

GramWasi is a social app where users share photos, videos, stories and messages with each other. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Data we collect

  • Account data: your username, display name, email address, and a securely hashed password (we never store or show your real password).
  • Profile data: profile photo, bio, followers and following lists.
  • Content you post: photos, videos, reels, stories, captions, comments and likes.
  • Messages: chat messages you send and receive inside the app.
  • Activity data: your last-active time (used to show "Active now" status), story views, and reports you submit.

How your data is used

  • To run the app: showing your posts, stories and messages to you and to other users.
  • To keep the community safe: handling reports and blocking.
  • We do not sell your personal data. We do not show third-party ads.

Where data is stored

App data is stored on our backend servers (hosted with Supabase). Photos and videos are additionally backed up using Telegram's cloud storage through our own storage bot, only so your content stays available and is not lost. Content is transmitted over HTTPS.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: In the app, open Settings → "Account delete karo". After confirming with your password, your account, posts, stories and messages are permanently deleted from our servers.
  • Block and report: You can block any user and report any post from inside the app.
  • Access: Everything you post is visible in your own profile inside the app.

Children

GramWasi is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
